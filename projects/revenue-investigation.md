# Revenue Investigation

A structured investigation of low-capital, automatable online business opportunities. Five waves produced 300 ideas, then progressively narrowed them through viability filtering, deeper investigation, implementation planning, competitor teardown, and final pre-build validation.

## Current status

Stage 5 development has **not started**.

After the Stage 4.5 competitor review and Stage 4.75 final validation design, only one concept remains ready for a behavioral market test without major redefinition:

### Canada Market Entry Snapshot

A low-cost, one-off report for a specific product / HS category combining:

- major Canadian importer names and locations from official public data;
- correctly preserved HS identifiers;
- current trade statistics and trends;
- geographic concentration;
- country-of-origin context where available;
- source dates and direct government citations; and
- clean XLSX / CSV output.

The product is intentionally positioned as a convenience and analysis layer over public Government of Canada data, not as proprietary or real-time shipment intelligence.

## Final validation gate

Before Stage 5, the concept must pass a real-money validation test using the intended inbound/self-serve acquisition model.

Proposed threshold:

- 10 narrow product / HS-category preview pages;
- one realistic sample Canada Market Entry Snapshot;
- CA$29 report offer;
- no cold-email dependency;
- maximum CA$75 exact-intent search-ad experiment;
- **GO:** at least 3 paid purchases within 75 qualified visitors, with at least 2 acquired without direct outreach;
- **REWORK:** 1–2 purchases or strong checkout / report engagement;
- **KILL:** 0 purchases after 75 qualified visitors.

## Concepts eliminated at the final gates

- **Excel / CSV leading-zero converter** — multiple strong free browser tools plus native Excel workarounds make the original paid wedge too weak.
- **Sponsor proof-of-delivery generator** — direct free and paid competitors already cover the core use case.
- **Product-manual metadata Actor** — a near-exact Apify competitor already exists at very low cost with weak public usage signals.
- **Product-spec contradiction checker** — current competitors now cover both Shopify-native contradiction detection and cross-source website / PDF audits closely enough to remove a clean entry wedge.
