# Revenue Investigation — Wave 38 Stage 1 Idea Pool

## Platform thesis
BigCommerce Marketplace supports paid installable apps and Unified Billing, which lets merchants purchase apps through the marketplace and roll charges into their BigCommerce invoice. Current automated-billing revenue share is 15% of net revenue.

## Constraint
Generate 60 narrow BigCommerce-native admin utilities. Avoid generic marketing, shipping, SEO, catalog sync, analytics, product feeds and broad migration services. Prefer read-only multi-storefront/channel change preflights and pricing/configuration safety tools. Stop at Stage 4 planning.
