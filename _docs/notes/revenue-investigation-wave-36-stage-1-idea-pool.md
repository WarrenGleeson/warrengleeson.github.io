# Revenue Investigation — Wave 36 Stage 1 Idea Pool

## Platform thesis
Wix App Market supports recurring and usage-based pricing, Wix-hosted checkout/billing, local currency and tax handling. Developers keep 100% of app revenue for the first 12 months, then 80% thereafter, after the transaction fee.

## Constraint
Generate 60 narrow Wix-native admin/lifecycle utilities. Avoid generic SEO, analytics, chat, forms, reviews, bookings, popups, email marketing and broad ecommerce apps. Prefer read-only preflights and change-safety tools that exploit Wix CMS, site, forms and business-solution APIs. Stop at Stage 4 planning.
