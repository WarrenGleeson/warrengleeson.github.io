# Wave 17 Stage 4 — Implementation Plans

Planning only. No customer testing, ads, payments, outreach campaigns or product development should be executed from this chat.

## Rank 1 — BC tenancy inspection procedural-evidence tracker

### Core promise
Help a BC landlord preserve a clean evidence trail around move-in/move-out inspection procedure and deadlines without replacing the official RTB inspection form.

### MVP plan
- Create tenancy/unit record.
- Record first inspection opportunity and tenant response.
- Track final-opportunity notice where required.
- Record inspection date, signatures and report completion.
- Calculate move-in copy deadline and move-out copy deadline based on forwarding-address timing.
- Record service/delivery evidence.
- Attach or link official inspection report and supporting photos.
- Export a procedural evidence timeline for the tenancy.
- Explicit disclaimer: workflow/evidence administration only, not legal advice.

### Likely stack
- Browser-first checklist/workspace.
- Deterministic date/deadline rules in versioned YAML/JSON.
- Optional PDF/email evidence upload.
- No need for full tenant portal or rent accounting.

### Pricing hypothesis
- Free: one active tenancy.
- CA$5–10: one completed inspection/turnover evidence pack.
- CA$10–20/month: small landlord/property-manager portfolio.

### Semi-automated acquisition pipeline
1. Monitor official BC RTB condition-inspection guidance and regulation changes.
2. Publish search pages around inspection deadlines, final-opportunity forms and report-copy timing.
3. Free one-tenancy checklist drives inbound acquisition.
4. Build synthetic examples of correct/incorrect timelines.
5. Track opt-in missed-step categories to improve reminders.
6. Partner/referral channel: landlord associations, small property managers and landlord-focused bookkeepers.
7. Any future direct outreach remains manual, low-volume and secondary to search/referrals.
8. Route portfolio/property-manager interest to the owner.

### Main risks
- Legal sensitivity and need for precise rule maintenance.
- Inspection apps could add procedural tracking.
- BC-only market unless later adapted province-by-province.

### Stage 4 verdict
**Highest-priority Wave 17 candidate.**

## Rank 2 — Rental turnover utility/key/meter evidence pack

### Core promise
Give small landlords one reliable handoff record for utilities, meter readings, keys/fobs, locks and vendor completion between tenants.

### MVP plan
- Property/unit turnover record.
- Record outgoing/incoming tenancy dates.
- Utility responsibility matrix and confirmation/reference numbers.
- Meter readings with timestamped photos.
- Key/fob/remote counts and return/reissue status.
- Lock/rekey completion evidence.
- Cleaning/repair completion checklist.
- Vacancy-period utility responsibility.
- Export owner/tenant handoff pack.

### Likely stack
- Mobile-friendly browser form.
- Photo/file upload.
- Simple PDF export.
- Optional reusable property template.

### Pricing hypothesis
- Free: one turnover.
- CA$5–10 per turnover.
- CA$8–15/month for a small portfolio.

### Semi-automated acquisition pipeline
1. Publish search content around utility transfer, meter readings, keys/fobs and rental turnover evidence.
2. Free downloadable/digital checklist is the funnel.
3. Template library by property type rather than complex integrations.
4. Track opt-in recurring problems to determine whether utilities, access devices or vendor handoffs create the strongest paid value.
5. Demonstrate with synthetic unit turnovers.
6. Partner potential: cleaners, small property managers and landlord associations.
7. Any future outreach should remain controlled; intended acquisition is search/template/referral driven.
8. Route repeat/multi-property usage to the owner.

### Main risks
- Easy spreadsheet/Notion substitution.
- Low price ceiling.
- Must be faster than a clipboard to win.

### Stage 4 verdict
**Useful broad landlord utility, but weaker economics than rank 1.**

## Rank 3 — Strata depreciation-report recommendation follow-through register

### Core promise
Turn a static BC strata depreciation report into a simple council action register that records what was actually repaired, replaced, deferred and spent before the next five-year update.

### MVP plan
- Import a structured CSV or manually enter report component rows; PDF-assisted import can be planned later.
- Track component, recommendation year, priority and estimated cost.
- Link council resolution/decision and project owner.
- Record quotes, selected vendor and approved budget.
- Record actual completion date/cost and variance.
- Attach invoices/photos/supporting evidence.
- Generate a “what changed since the last report” export for future report-provider handoff.
- Do not generate reserve funding advice or a legally compliant depreciation report.

### Likely stack
- Lightweight spreadsheet-style SaaS.
- CSV import/export first.
- Optional PDF extraction after formats are understood.
- No owner portal/accounting module required.

### Pricing hypothesis
- CA$20–40 one-off report setup/import.
- CA$10–25/month per strata.
- Consultant/report-provider partner tier later.

### Semi-automated acquisition pipeline
1. Publish practical content around “what to do after a depreciation report.”
2. Free report-to-roadmap template serves as the acquisition funnel.
3. Partner channel: depreciation-report providers who do not already supply a living planning platform.
4. Target self-managed strata councils and small management firms rather than large professional managers.
5. Demonstrate using synthetic report components/projects.
6. Track opt-in request patterns to decide whether PDF import or council-resolution tracking deserves priority.
7. Any direct outreach in a future chat should be manual and partner-oriented rather than mass council prospecting.
8. Route multiple-strata/consultant demand to the owner.

### Main risks
- Existing strata/reserve tools already cover parts of this workflow.
- Volunteer councils may resist another system.
- PDF report structures vary widely.

### Stage 4 verdict
**Viable as a deliberately simple vendor-neutral follow-through tool; more competitive than ranks 1–2.**

## Wave 17 conclusion

Top three:

1. BC tenancy inspection procedural-evidence tracker
2. Rental turnover utility/key/meter evidence pack
3. Strata depreciation-report recommendation follow-through register

Wave 17 confirms that property management contains smaller evidence and handoff problems that sit between paper checklists and full property-management platforms, particularly for small landlords and self-managed strata councils.
