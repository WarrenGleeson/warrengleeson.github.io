# Wave 39 Stage 2 — Viability Filter

## Evidence that changed scoring

- Notion Marketplace supports native paid templates and Notion acts as merchant of record, handling payments, sales tax/VAT, refunds, fraud monitoring and buyer support.
- Current native fee is 8% + US$0.40 per transaction; non-US creators may also incur a 1% FX fee.
- Marketplace saturation is extreme: 30,000+ templates, including 1,000+ SOP templates and large numbers of vendor-management, contract, forms, audit and offboarding templates.
- Generic SaaS renewal tracking is already directly served by notice-period/renewal trackers, including free products.
- Generic scope-change, client offboarding, RFP and audit checklists all have current exact competitors.
- Notion editorial guidance explicitly favors templates that solve a specific need, show novelty and use richer capabilities such as databases, buttons, formulas and embeds.
- The strongest remaining opportunities are narrow decision systems where linked evidence, scoring, approvals and next-step logic make the product meaningfully more than a tracker.
