# Wave 20 Stage 4 — Implementation Plans

Planning only. No customer testing, ads, payments, outreach campaigns or product development should be executed from this chat.

## Rank 1 — BC society AGM-to-filing evidence tracker

### Core promise
Help a small BC society move from AGM to completed annual filing with a clean evidence trail that the next board/treasurer can understand.

### MVP plan
- Create annual governance cycle record.
- Record AGM date and calculate 30-day filing deadline.
- Capture director roster before/after AGM.
- Checklist: notice, quorum, minutes, resolutions, financial statements presented, director changes.
- Record annual-report filing date/reference and director-change filing status.
- Store/link key governance records.
- Produce year-end governance handoff pack.
- Never replace BC Registries filing or legal advice.

### Likely stack
- Browser-first checklist/workspace.
- Deterministic BC deadline rules.
- File/link attachments.
- Simple annual PDF/ZIP handoff export.

### Pricing hypothesis
- Free: one annual cycle/checklist.
- CA$10–20: completed annual governance pack.
- CA$8–15/month: recurring history/reminders for societies with more activity.

### Semi-automated acquisition pipeline
1. Monitor BC Societies Act/Registry guidance for filing/deadline changes.
2. Publish exact-intent pages around AGM annual-report deadlines and director changes.
3. Free annual checklist is the inbound funnel.
4. Demonstrate using a fictional volunteer society.
5. Partner channel: nonprofit bookkeepers, accountants and society-governance consultants.
6. Track opt-in missed-step categories to refine workflow.
7. Any future direct outreach remains manual and limited; search/referrals are preferred.
8. Route multi-client accountant/bookkeeper interest to the owner.

### Main risks
- BC-only and annual-use market.
- Organizations may accept spreadsheets/manual reminders.
- Governance/legal boundaries require careful wording.

### Stage 4 verdict
**Highest-priority Wave 20 candidate.**

## Rank 2 — T3010 filing-readiness evidence preflight

### Core promise
Give a small Canadian charity a clean annual handoff package showing whether the administrative information and supporting records needed to begin its T3010 filing are assembled before the six-month deadline.

### MVP plan
- Record fiscal year-end and calculate T3010 deadline.
- Record charity number, current directors/officers and contact details.
- Checklist for financial statements, prior return, activity changes and relevant attachments/schedules based on user input.
- Record preparer/accountant assignment and handoff status.
- Missing-document queue and reminder schedule.
- Archive final filing confirmation/reference.
- Produce a preparer handoff index.
- Do not calculate return values, determine tax treatment or submit the return.

### Likely stack
- Lightweight browser workspace.
- Versioned CRA checklist/deadline rules.
- Optional accountant multi-client dashboard later.

### Pricing hypothesis
- Free: deadline/basic checklist.
- CA$10–25: annual readiness/handoff pack.
- Accountant/bookkeeper plan for multiple charity clients.

### Semi-automated acquisition pipeline
1. Monitor CRA T3010 filing guidance and the planned 2027 online-filing transition.
2. Publish search pages around T3010 deadlines, annual preparation and board/treasurer handoff.
3. Free deadline calculator/checklist is the inbound funnel.
4. Demonstrate with synthetic charity records only.
5. Partner channel: nonprofit accountants/bookkeepers and charity consultants.
6. Track opt-in missing-item categories to refine checklist quality.
7. Future outreach should target professional intermediaries rather than mass-contacting charities.
8. Route multi-charity preparer demand to the owner.

### Main risks
- Annual/seasonal usage.
- CRA's online filing may absorb more readiness guidance.
- Must remain clearly outside tax/legal advice.

### Stage 4 verdict
**Good small annual-compliance utility, especially through accountant/bookkeeper channels.**

## Rank 3 — Grant amendment-to-obligation diff

### Core promise
Show a nonprofit exactly what operational obligations changed when a funder issues an amendment to an existing grant/contribution agreement.

### MVP plan
- Upload original agreement and amendment/revised agreement.
- Extract dated obligations, deliverables, reporting deadlines, budget categories and attachment/evidence requirements.
- Compare before/after and classify: added, removed, changed, unchanged.
- Link each finding to exact source pages/sections.
- Manual-review category for ambiguous/legal clauses.
- Export change checklist with owner/status fields.
- Do not provide legal interpretation or determine enforceability.

### Likely stack
- Browser upload utility.
- PDF/text extraction plus rules-first diffing.
- Optional language-model assistance for candidate extraction with citations and human review.

### Pricing hypothesis
- CA$10–20 per amendment comparison.
- CA$10–25/month for organizations with multiple active grants/contracts.

### Semi-automated acquisition pipeline
1. Publish content around grant amendments and contribution-agreement change control.
2. Free synthetic example diff demonstrates the value.
3. Partner channel: grant consultants, nonprofit finance leads and Indigenous/community organization advisors.
4. Keep the initial document scope narrow to common agreement/amendment structures.
5. Track opt-in clause/change categories to improve extraction.
6. Future validation should use redacted or synthetic agreements until privacy/security design is established.
7. Any outreach remains partner-led/manual.
8. Route multi-agreement teams to the owner.

### Main risks
- Document variability.
- Legal nuance can make extraction misleading if confidence is overstated.
- Full grant platforms can add amendment-diff features.

### Stage 4 verdict
**Useful narrow document utility with moderate implementation risk.**
