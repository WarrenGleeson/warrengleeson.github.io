# Wave 20 Stage 2 — Viability Filter

## Evidence that changed scoring

- BC societies must maintain specific governance records and file an annual report within 30 days after the AGM. That creates a concrete deadline/evidence workflow for small volunteer-run organizations.
- Canadian registered charities must file a complete T3010 within six months of fiscal year-end; failure to file can lead to revocation. CRA is also moving toward fully online T3010 filing in 2027.
- Generic grant management is crowded, with mature products covering deadlines, budgets, evidence and reporting.
- Association-management systems already cover dues, chapters, committees, events and continuing-education credits.
- Credential management is also mature across LMS/association platforms.
- Sponsor fulfillment tracking is increasingly served by purpose-built workflows and templates, so the best wedge is evidence/reconciliation rather than task tracking itself.
