# Wave 24 Stage 2 — Viability Filter

## Evidence that changed scoring

- Restaurant food-cost/invoice/vendor tools are mature and increasingly affordable for independents; generic supplier-cost monitoring is not differentiated.
- Delivery marketplace reconciliation is already available in restaurant operations platforms that match POS orders, marketplace payouts, commissions, refunds and bank deposits.
- Hotel OTA commission/VCC reconciliation is a mature specialist category, including contingency/pay-for-performance offerings.
- Catering/BEO/final-invoice reconciliation now has an exact current product that compares BEOs, agreements, changes, guarantees and supplier invoices.
- Hotel housekeeping, maintenance and out-of-order room visibility are mature categories, including PMS-native blocked-room dashboards.
- Early check-in and late checkout are meaningful ancillary-revenue products, yet posting/waiver handling varies by PMS. Some systems automate them, while others can still require manual check-out-day charge handling.
- Menu synchronization middleware exists, but many independents still maintain multiple disconnected copies across POS, website and delivery channels; an independent drift audit can be lighter than migrating into middleware.
