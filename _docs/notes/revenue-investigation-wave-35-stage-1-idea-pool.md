# Revenue Investigation — Wave 35 Stage 1 Idea Pool

## Platform thesis

Microsoft Teams apps published to the Teams Store are also listed in Microsoft Marketplace. Teams apps can be linked to transactable SaaS offers, with Microsoft handling customer billing; current Marketplace service fee is 3%. This supports recurring flat-rate or per-user pricing with enterprise procurement benefits.

## Constraint

Generate 60 platform-native opportunities for Teams / Microsoft 365. Avoid generic meeting bots, AI assistants, project management, CRM, broad governance suites, compliance/eDiscovery platforms and generic workflow builders. Focus on narrow admin continuity, lifecycle and change-risk jobs. Stop at Stage 4 planning.
