# Revenue Investigation — Wave 33 Stage 1 Idea Pool

## Platform thesis

Salesforce AgentExchange/AppExchange provides enterprise discovery and checkout, but economics are heavier than Figma/Shopify/GitHub. Current ISVforce revenue share is 15%, paid app listings have an annual fee, and managed-package security review is a meaningful upfront cost. Therefore Wave 33 prioritizes higher-value admin utilities rather than low-priced micro-apps.

## Constraint

Generate 60 Salesforce-native opportunities. Avoid generic dashboards, duplicate cleanup, permission reporting, field cleanup, broad metadata impact analysis, backup, CPQ, e-signature and generic Agentforce agents. Focus on platform-change readiness, integration lifecycle and operational transition checks. Stop at Stage 4 planning.
