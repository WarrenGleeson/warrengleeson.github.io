# Wave 27 Stage 2 — Viability Filter

## Evidence that changed scoring

- BC Hydro's customer-generation process explicitly warns that incomplete or inconsistent submissions can delay or reclassify applications, and final stages require inspection/contractor documents plus paid invoice evidence.
- Solar compliance/interconnection package generation is already being automated by current products, including document compliance, regulatory tracking and cross-document interconnection validation.
- Commissioning evidence software is also current and specialized for solar PV, EV and BMS projects.
- EV charger rebate programs remain document-heavy: pre-approval, paid/itemized invoices, contractor installation forms, inspections, charger model details, networking proof and other supporting documents can be required depending on program/project type.
- The better opportunity is not another design/commissioning platform but independent **cross-document consistency and final-package QA** for small installers using mixed portals/files.
