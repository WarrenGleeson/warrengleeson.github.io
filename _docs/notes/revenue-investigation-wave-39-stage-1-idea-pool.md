# Revenue Investigation — Wave 39 Stage 1 Idea Pool

## Platform thesis
Notion Marketplace supports paid templates with native discovery and checkout. Notion is merchant of record, handles regional tax/VAT, purchase support, refunds and fraud monitoring, and currently charges 8% + US$0.40 per transaction. The marketplace is very large, so generic planners, CRMs, SOPs, vendor trackers and dashboards are poor targets.

## Constraint
Generate 60 narrow paid-template opportunities that behave more like mini operational systems: multiple linked databases, formulas, buttons, guided workflows, sample data and decision views. Prefer problems with a concrete business consequence and low ongoing support. Stop at Stage 4 planning.
