# Revenue Investigation — Wave 31 Stage 1 Idea Pool

## Platform thesis

Figma classic plugins can be sold directly through Community in supported countries, with Figma handling purchase support, delivery, refunds, tax and fraud. Canva public apps receive Marketplace distribution; monetization is available through its approval-based Premium Apps Program or external payment links.

## Constraint

Generate 60 narrow plugin/app ideas. Avoid generic icon libraries, stock assets, AI copywriters, background removal, simple export tools, QR generators, basic layer renaming and broad accessibility/design linting. Prefer small utilities with obvious in-editor value and low support burden. Stop at Stage 4 planning.
