# Wave 39 Stage 3 — Deep Investigation

## 1. Vendor price-increase decision pack

### Problem
SMBs often receive vendor renewal/price-increase notices with little structure for deciding whether to accept, negotiate, downgrade, replace or exit. Generic vendor trackers record dates and spend, but do not turn a proposed increase into a decision process.

### Product
A paid Notion template with linked databases for vendors, contracts, alternatives, decision evidence and actions. Core workflow:
- record current price, proposed price, increase %, notice deadline and annualized impact;
- classify criticality, switching cost, data/access dependency and replacement feasibility;
- compare alternatives on migration effort, feature gaps and expected savings;
- log negotiation concessions and final offer;
- calculate stay/switch/exit scenarios;
- require owner, recommendation, approval and next action;
- generate a concise decision page for each increase.

### Distribution
Notion Marketplace search around vendor management, SaaS costs, procurement, contract renewal and startup operations.

### Monetization hypothesis
One-time paid template around US$15–39, with a premium version containing richer formulas, sample scenarios and negotiation/decision playbooks.

### Competition
Generic vendor-management and renewal trackers are common. Exact competitor review did not surface a template centered specifically on **price-increase decision analysis** rather than renewal-date tracking.

### Verdict
**Advance.** Best Wave 39 template niche.

## 2. Quote-to-contract variance tracker

### Problem
For small procurement and service-buying teams, the commercial promise in a quote/proposal can drift before the final contract is signed: price, scope, quantities, service levels, implementation dates, renewal language, included support and assumptions can change. Generic contract trackers do not preserve the decision trail between quote and contract.

### Product
A linked Notion system for one procurement/deal process:
- quote/proposal terms database;
- final-contract terms database;
- requirement/term comparison table;
- variance classification: expected, negotiated, unexplained, unfavorable;
- financial impact formula;
- owner/reviewer/approval fields;
- evidence links and decision notes;
- open-variance queue before signature;
- post-signature handoff checklist.

No legal determination is made; users decide whether a variance matters.

### Distribution
Marketplace search around procurement, vendor selection, contract management, purchasing and operations.

### Monetization hypothesis
US$12–35 one-time, with example scenarios for agencies, SaaS purchases and service contracts.

### Competition
RFP, proposal and contract trackers exist, but current review did not surface a focused **quote-to-final-contract variance workspace**.

### Verdict
**Advance.** Clear business consequence and easy-to-understand listing.

## 3. System-owner departure continuity room

### Problem
When a staff member leaves, the risk is not only their login. They may own recurring reports, automations, vendor relationships, domains, billing accounts, dashboards, API integrations, documentation and operational decisions. HR offboarding templates are common, but they rarely map the systems/processes that lose an accountable owner.

### Product
A transition workspace centered on operational ownership rather than HR tasks:
- systems/processes owned by departing person;
- vendor/account/admin-role dependencies;
- automations/reports/jobs they maintain;
- renewal/billing/contact responsibilities;
- successor owner and acceptance status;
- knowledge/evidence handoff checklist;
- unresolved single-owner risks;
- cutover date and post-departure verification queue.

### Distribution
Marketplace search around employee offboarding, operations, IT handoff, knowledge transfer and business continuity.

### Monetization hypothesis
US$15–39 one-time template, targeting founders/ops/IT teams rather than HR departments.

### Competition
Employee offboarding templates are common, and some operational-remnant templates exist. The surviving wedge is **ownership continuity across business systems/processes before departure**, not account-remnant discovery after departure.

### Verdict
**Advance cautiously.** Good differentiation if positioned tightly.
