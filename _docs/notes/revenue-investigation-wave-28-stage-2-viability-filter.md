# Wave 28 Stage 2 — Viability Filter

## Evidence that changed scoring

- Telecom expense management and invoice auditing are mature categories, including products marketed to small businesses.
- Microsoft 365/license reconciliation now has exact MSP products comparing distributor seats, tenant counts and PSA/client billing, so generic seat reconciliation is crowded.
- MSP PSA/ticketing products already include SLA tracking, but vendor-side service-credit recovery remains different: many SaaS/carrier SLAs require the customer/MSP to file a claim within a short deadline and preserve specific incident evidence.
- Microsoft Partner Center SLA credits require customer impact evidence, outage incident ID, tenant ID and timely submission; telecom carriers can similarly require trouble-ticket IDs, circuit/site details and claims within days.
- MSP offboarding guidance consistently requires removal/reconciliation of privileged access, agents, delegated relationships, licenses and vendor costs, creating a cross-system closeout gap even where checklists exist.
- Telecom carrier billing can begin/end on service-order milestones that may not align cleanly with actual activation/disconnection evidence, creating a narrow reconciliation opportunity outside full TEM platforms.
