# Wave 17 Stage 2 — Viability Filter

## Evidence that changed scoring

- BC condition-inspection procedure has concrete deadlines and evidence requirements: landlords must offer two inspection opportunities, use the approved final-opportunity process where required, complete/sign the report, and provide copies within prescribed timeframes. Failure can extinguish the landlord's right to claim against deposits for damage.
- Move-in/move-out inspection software is already crowded, including tenant-guided, staff-guided and AI comparison products. The viable wedge is procedural evidence and deadline tracking, not another inspection/photo app.
- Small-landlord turnover utilities (utility transfers, meter readings, keys/fobs, rekeying, vendor completion) are often handled as checklists rather than dedicated systems. This is operationally real but has lower willingness to pay.
- BC strata depreciation reports are now mandatory on five-year cycles for stratas with five or more lots. However, reserve-planning providers and strata platforms increasingly digitize the report, funding scenarios and follow-through, weakening generic depreciation-report trackers.
- Commercial CAM reconciliation and tenant-insurance/certificate tracking are already mature software categories.
