# Distribution, Collaborations, and Ecosystem

## Objective

Reach relevant audiences outside the existing follower base through selective, low-volume distribution partnerships.

## Questions to answer

- Which Irish civic, academic, media, policy, student, or community accounts overlap with EirePolitic's informational mission?
- Which partnership types preserve neutrality?
- Where are Collab posts appropriate?
- When are mentions/tags useful rather than spammy?
- What outreach can be done in small batches?

## Potential partner classes

- civic education organisations;
- academic/policy institutes;
- journalism/media accounts;
- university politics/law/public-policy societies;
- local-information/community organisations;
- non-partisan public-information initiatives.

## Investigation tasks

- [ ] Build a small ecosystem map.
- [ ] Define neutrality-screening criteria.
- [ ] Rank partnership formats by effort and reach potential.
- [ ] Define a low-volume outreach process.
- [ ] Test 1-2 collaboration formats.
- [ ] Measure incremental reach and follower conversion.

## Success criteria

- incremental non-follower reach;
- follows attributed to collaboration periods/posts;
- profile visits;
- recurring partner interest;
- low manual workload.

## Risks / safeguards

Avoid patterns that make EirePolitic appear affiliated with or supportive of political parties, campaigns, or ideological organisations.

## Handoff instructions for future LLM chats

Use current web research to identify live accounts and organisations. Keep recommendations descriptive and neutrality-aware. Do not automate unsolicited mass outreach.
