# Experiment Programme and 30/60/90-Day Plan

## Objective

Turn the individual growth workstreams into a measurable sequence of small experiments and implementation tasks.

## Experiment principles

- Change as few variables as practical at once.
- Label variants in structured metadata where possible.
- Prefer repeatable tests over subjective impressions.
- Use acquisition metrics, not likes, as the primary decision criteria.
- Avoid prematurely declaring success from one unusually strong post.
- Record negative results and abandoned ideas.

## Standard experiment record

Each experiment should document:

- hypothesis;
- content family;
- control;
- treatment;
- start/end dates;
- sample size;
- primary metric;
- secondary metrics;
- minimum success criterion;
- result;
- interpretation;
- follow-up action.

## Candidate early experiments

- formal vs general-audience cover headline;
- question vs factual hook;
- short vs long caption;
- topical vs procedural framing;
- carousel vs automated Reel;
- CTA vs no CTA;
- different carousel depth;
- different posting-time windows;
- topic category comparisons.

## Initial 30-day focus

Expected emphasis:
- measurement baseline;
- profile audit;
- first cover/hook tests;
- experiment metadata conventions.

## Initial 60-day focus

Expected emphasis:
- automated repurposing tests;
- share/save-oriented formats;
- topical/evergreen mix;
- early collaboration/distribution tests.

## Initial 90-day focus

Expected emphasis:
- consolidate winning formats;
- automate proven variants;
- retire low-value work;
- formalise recurring reporting;
- decide whether any paid promotion experiment is justified.

## Prioritisation framework

For each candidate initiative, record:

- expected upside;
- confidence;
- implementation effort;
- recurring manual effort;
- monetary cost;
- automation potential;
- neutrality/credibility risk;
- dependencies.

## Handoff instructions for future LLM chats

Do not freeze the 30/60/90-day plan before the measurement workstream establishes a baseline. Update this page as evidence accumulates from the other workstreams.
