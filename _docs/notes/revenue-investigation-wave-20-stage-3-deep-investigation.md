# Wave 20 Stage 3 — Deep Investigation

## 1. BC society AGM-to-filing evidence tracker

### Problem
Small BC societies are often volunteer-run. The Societies Act requires governance records to be maintained and an annual report to be filed within 30 days after the AGM. The actual filing is simple; the operational risk is losing track of whether the AGM occurred, directors changed, minutes/resolutions are complete, the annual report was filed, and the evidence is preserved for the next board.

### Product
A lightweight annual-governance workflow:
- AGM date and filing deadline;
- director/officer roster before/after AGM;
- minutes/resolution checklist;
- financial-statement presentation evidence;
- registry filing date/reference;
- director-change filing status;
- required-record index;
- next-board handoff pack.

The product does not file with BC Registries or provide legal advice.

### Distribution
Search around BC society annual reports, AGM requirements and board handovers; nonprofit accountants/bookkeepers and society consultants.

### Monetization hypothesis
Free one-year checklist; CA$10–20 annual evidence pack; CA$8–15/month for organizations wanting recurring governance reminders/history.

### Competition
General board portals and nonprofit governance products exist, but the current review did not identify a strong lightweight BC-specific product centered on the annual AGM-to-registry evidence cycle.

### Risk
Very narrow geography and low annual transaction frequency.

### Verdict
**Advance.** Strongest Wave 20 concept.

## 2. T3010 filing-readiness evidence preflight

### Problem
Every Canadian registered charity must file a complete T3010 within six months after fiscal year-end, and failure to file can lead to revocation. CRA is also moving toward fully online filing in 2027. Small charities often rely on a volunteer treasurer/accountant handoff and need to know whether the annual information and supporting documents are ready before filing begins.

### Product
A non-tax-advisory readiness workspace:
- fiscal year-end and filing deadline;
- current director/officer/contact information;
- financial statement availability;
- prior-year return/reference;
- required schedules/attachments checklist based on user-selected facts;
- organization/activity-change review prompts;
- preparer/accountant handoff index;
- filing confirmation archive.

It does not calculate tax positions, determine charity-law treatment or submit the T3010.

### Distribution
Search around T3010 deadlines/checklists, nonprofit bookkeepers/accountants and charity-board communities.

### Monetization hypothesis
Free deadline/checklist; CA$10–25 annual readiness pack; accountant/bookkeeper multi-client tier later.

### Competition
Tax/accounting software and charity-management products may cover parts of this, but the current review did not surface a strong low-cost **preparer handoff/readiness layer** for very small charities.

### Risk
Annual-use product with limited direct recurring revenue; tax/legal boundaries must be explicit.

### Verdict
**Advance.** Good annual compliance-administration niche.

## 3. Grant amendment-to-obligation diff

### Problem
After a grant is awarded, amendments can change deadlines, budget categories, deliverables, reporting dates or evidence expectations. Generic grant software stores the current award but small teams can miss exactly what changed between the original agreement and amendment.

### Product
Upload an original contribution/grant agreement plus amendment. The tool produces a review-only diff focused on operational fields:
- changed dates/deadlines;
- budget/eligible-cost changes;
- deliverable changes;
- reporting-frequency changes;
- changed attachment/evidence requirements;
- clauses requiring manual review.

Each finding links to the original/amended source text. It does not interpret legal effect.

### Distribution
Search around grant amendments/change orders, nonprofit grant managers, Indigenous/community organizations and grant consultants.

### Monetization hypothesis
CA$10–20 per amendment comparison or CA$10–25/month for organizations with multiple active contribution agreements.

### Competition
Grant-management platforms track award changes broadly, but the current gap is a small **agreement-to-amendment operational diff** that can be used without adopting a grant platform.

### Risk
Document structures vary and legal nuance is high. The output must be framed as change detection, not interpretation.

### Verdict
**Advance cautiously.** Clear administrative value but smaller market.

## Dropped after deeper review

### Chapter membership/remittance reconciliation
Current chapter-management products already aggregate dues, chapter member counts and remittances centrally.

### Sponsor payment-vs-fulfillment tracking
Current sponsorship products and nonprofit workflows already separate payment status from deliverable fulfillment and proof of performance.

### Generic grant reporting/evidence management
Mature grant platforms already cover deadlines, budgets, deliverables, evidence and reports.

### Credential/CE tracking
Association-management and learning platforms already manage credits, certificates and renewal cycles.

## Stage 3 ranking

1. BC society AGM-to-filing evidence tracker
2. T3010 filing-readiness evidence preflight
3. Grant amendment-to-obligation diff

## Advance to Stage 4

Advance those three only.
