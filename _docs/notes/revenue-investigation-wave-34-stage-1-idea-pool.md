# Revenue Investigation — Wave 34 Stage 1 Idea Pool

## Platform thesis
Woo Marketplace supports paid standalone extensions with Woo-hosted licensing/billing and recurring subscription reporting. Marketplace review favors a single core purpose, out-of-box operation and compatibility with major Woo extensions.

## Constraint
Generate 60 narrow WooCommerce admin utilities. Avoid payments, subscriptions, shipping-rate providers, SEO, reviews, checkout builders, product feeds, generic inventory, generic store-health and webhook-monitoring products. Prefer read-only preflights and change simulation. Stop at Stage 4 planning.
