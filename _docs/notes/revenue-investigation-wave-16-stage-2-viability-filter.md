# Wave 16 Stage 2 — Viability Filter

## Evidence that changed scoring

- Generic T&M field-ticket capture is already well served by dedicated products that collect labour, equipment, materials, signatures and billing records. The better surviving wedge is independent reconciliation of approved tickets against invoices/rate schedules.
- HVAC commissioning, O&M handover and QR asset records are also mature categories, including products designed specifically for startup, handover and warranty evidence.
- BC Hydro rebate programs create highly structured documentation requirements. Current EV-charger applications can require itemized paid invoices, permits, photos, inspection evidence and contractor forms; heat-pump programs also require invoice fields and, for some projects, heat-load calculation documents.
- WorkSafeBC explicitly recommends obtaining clearance letters before and after subcontracted work because a hiring business can become liable for unpaid premiums. Its public tool can generate letters for up to 150 firms, but ongoing contract-period evidence/expiry management remains an administrative workflow.
- Rebate and clearance workflows are narrower than full contractor CRMs and can potentially be delivered as self-serve document checks.
