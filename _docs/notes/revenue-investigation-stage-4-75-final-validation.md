# Revenue Investigation — Stage 4.75 Final Validation Gate

## Outcome

The final competitor pass eliminated the cross-source product-spec concept from immediate consideration. A current product, SpecDrift, already offers website/PDF contradiction audits with exact source evidence, a free single-model check, and a low-cost pilot.

That leaves one concept worth behavioral validation before Stage 5:

## Canada Market Entry Snapshot

A low-cost, one-off report for a specific product / HS category combining:

- major Canadian importer names and locations from official public data;
- correctly preserved HS identifiers;
- current Canadian trade statistics and trends;
- geographic concentration;
- country-of-origin context where available;
- source dates and direct government citations; and
- clean XLSX / CSV output.

The product must be positioned as a convenience and analysis layer over public data, not as proprietary or real-time shipment intelligence.

## Why this version survives

The Canadian Importers Database provides useful free raw information, while professional trade-intelligence subscriptions are expensive. The remaining opportunity is not to replace those systems; it is to serve buyers who want a fast, transparent, one-off market snapshot without learning government datasets or buying a subscription.

## Final behavioral validation

Do not build a database or SaaS product yet.

### Test setup

1. Select 10 narrow product / HS categories with meaningful importer activity.
2. Produce factual preview pages using official Canadian data.
3. Create one realistic sample Canada Market Entry Snapshot.
4. Offer the report at **CA$29**.
5. Fulfil any early purchases manually from official datasets.
6. Acquire traffic primarily through indexable pages and, if needed, a maximum **CA$75 exact-intent search-ad experiment**.
7. Do not rely on cold-email prospecting for the test.

## Precommitted decision thresholds

### GO

Proceed to Stage 5 only if:

- at least **3 paid purchases** occur within **75 qualified visitors**; and
- at least **2 purchases** come from inbound/self-serve acquisition rather than direct outreach.

### REWORK

Allow one pricing/package revision if:

- 1–2 purchases occur; or
- there is strong preview/checkout engagement but insufficient completed purchases.

### KILL

Do not proceed to Stage 5 if:

- there are **0 purchases after 75 qualified visitors**.

Do not reinterpret a failed threshold as validation for a larger software build.

## Stage 5 boundary

Stage 5 is still **not authorized** by this document. Stage 5 begins only after the behavioral test demonstrates willingness to pay under the precommitted threshold.
