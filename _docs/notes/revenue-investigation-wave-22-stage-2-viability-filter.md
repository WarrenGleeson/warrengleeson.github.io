# Wave 22 Stage 2 — Viability Filter

## Evidence that changed scoring

- Alberta private career colleges must keep detailed student records and are subject to desk audits and inspections. Annual reporting is mandatory, and attendance records are part of the required student file.
- Alberta Student Aid withdrawal rules make attendance monitoring operationally important: five consecutive unexcused class days can trigger withdrawal, while other prolonged absence conditions can also require withdrawal reporting.
- A dedicated Alberta PCC audit-ready SIS already exists, so another full student-record platform is not differentiated.
- Alberta childcare monthly claims are subject to review/audit and require backup documentation including attendance records, ECE timesheets/payroll and child registration records.
- Canadian childcare-management software now includes provincial claim generation and subsidy-hour reconciliation, so a full claims platform is already competitive.
- The Office of the Auditor General found claim discrepancies in more than half of the sampled Alberta childcare operators, supporting demand for independent pre-submission/review QA.
- Generic CE/LMS/certificate-management tooling is mature; narrow reconciliation/audit tools are more plausible than another learning platform.
