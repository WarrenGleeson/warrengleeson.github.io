# Wave 39 Stage 4 — Implementation Plans

Planning only. No template creation, Marketplace submission, customer testing, payments or outreach should be executed from this chat.

## Rank 1 — Vendor price-increase decision pack

### Product design
- Databases: Vendors, Contracts, Alternatives, Negotiation Events, Decisions, Actions.
- Formula fields: annualized increase, increase %, cancel-by date, estimated switching cost, annual savings/loss, net first-year impact.
- Views: increases needing decision, deadlines in 30/60/90 days, negotiate, replace, accept, exit.
- Buttons/templates for starting a price-increase review, adding an alternative and recording a negotiated offer.
- Pre-filled examples for SaaS, logistics/service provider and agency/vendor cases.
- Decision page summarizes current/proposed economics, evidence, options, recommendation, approver and next action.

### Pricing/distribution
- Target US$15–39 one-time on Notion Marketplace.
- Native Notion payment is preferred: Notion is merchant of record and currently charges 8% + US$0.40 per transaction.
- Listing keywords: vendor price increase, SaaS cost, procurement, renewal negotiation, vendor management.
- Screenshots must show the decision page, not a generic dashboard.

### Validation plan for separate chat
- Compare exact top Marketplace search results for vendor/contract renewal and procurement.
- Test whether title/search wording exposes the price-increase intent.
- Build only after confirming no exact paid competitor has emerged.

### Stage 4 verdict
**Best Wave 39 candidate for separate validation/build.**

## Rank 2 — Quote-to-contract variance tracker

### Product design
- Databases: Deals/Purchases, Quoted Terms, Contract Terms, Variances, Evidence, Approvals.
- Standard comparison dimensions: price, quantity, scope, dates, implementation, support, SLA, renewal, termination, assumptions.
- Formula for financial delta and unresolved-variance count.
- Views: signature blockers, unfavorable variances, approved deviations, post-signature handoff.
- Reusable comparison page per vendor/deal.
- Explicit disclaimer that the template organizes commercial differences and does not provide legal advice.

### Pricing/distribution
- Target US$12–35 one-time.
- Marketplace keywords: quote comparison, contract review, procurement, vendor contract, proposal comparison.
- Use realistic fictional quote-to-contract examples to make the value obvious from screenshots.

### Validation plan for separate chat
- Search current procurement/contract templates for exact variance workflows.
- Test demand among small agencies/operations/procurement users rather than enterprise legal teams.

### Stage 4 verdict
**Strong narrow decision product with low support burden.**

## Rank 3 — System-owner departure continuity room

### Product design
- Databases: People, Systems, Processes, Vendors, Automations/Recurring Jobs, Responsibilities, Handoff Tasks.
- Relations map every owned system/process to current and successor owner.
- Formula/rollup flags objects with no accepted successor.
- Views: single-owner risks, admin/billing responsibilities, recurring jobs, unresolved handoff, post-departure verification.
- Departure template creates the full handoff checklist automatically from linked responsibilities.

### Pricing/distribution
- Target US$15–39 one-time.
- Marketplace keywords: employee offboarding, knowledge transfer, business continuity, IT handoff, system owner.
- Position away from HR checklists: “what operational systems lose an owner when this person leaves?”

### Validation plan for separate chat
- Re-check recent offboarding/IT continuity products, especially AI Skills.
- Confirm the ownership-continuity framing remains distinct from account-remnant audits.

### Stage 4 verdict
**Good niche, but competition from fast-growing AI Skills makes it less durable than ranks 1–2.**

## Platform economics note

Notion Marketplace is easy to enter compared with app ecosystems because the product is a self-contained template and native payments are available. Notion is merchant of record and handles sales tax/VAT, purchase support, refunds and fraud monitoring. Current payment processing through Notion costs creators 8% + US$0.40 per transaction; creators outside the US can also incur a 1% FX payout fee.

The main constraint is saturation, not platform friction. Notion has 30,000+ templates and thousands of creators, so generic planners, SOPs, CRMs, vendor trackers, audits, forms and dashboards should be avoided.

## Wave 39 conclusion

Top three:
1. Vendor price-increase decision pack
2. Quote-to-contract variance tracker
3. System-owner departure continuity room

Notion is attractive as a low-capital platform, but the winning strategy differs from app stores: build narrowly scoped operational decision products that are immediately useful after duplication and can justify a paid purchase without ongoing SaaS infrastructure.
