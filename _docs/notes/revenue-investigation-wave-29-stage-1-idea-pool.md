# Revenue Investigation — Wave 29 Stage 1 Idea Pool

## Platform thesis

Atlassian Marketplace provides built-in listing, licensing and Paid via Atlassian billing. Forge apps currently qualify for 100% of gross Marketplace revenue up to the first US$1M in lifetime Forge revenue, making the ecosystem attractive for small product-led utilities.

## Constraint

Generate 60 narrow Forge-app opportunities for Jira, Jira Service Management and Confluence. Avoid mature categories such as generic workflow scripting, reporting, time tracking, test management, stale-content review, broken links, custom-field cleanup, permission audits, automation maps, release readiness and broad configuration management. Stop at Stage 4 planning.
