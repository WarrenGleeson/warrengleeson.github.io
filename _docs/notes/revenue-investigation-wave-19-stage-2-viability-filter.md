# Wave 19 Stage 2 — Viability Filter

## Evidence that changed scoring

- Canadian traceability rules require identifiable food/lot information plus one-step-back and one-step-forward records; electronic records must be accessible in Canada and provided in a single file in a standard usable format when requested.
- Generic traceability, mock-recall, PCP/HACCP and inspection-binder products are already crowded, including Canadian products aimed specifically at small processors.
- Free tools now trace lots from CSVs and audit approved-supplier spreadsheets, reducing the value of generic spreadsheet checkers.
- Supplier-management systems already maintain approved supplier lists, certificates and purchase workflows. A surviving narrower wedge is an independent **actual-purchases vs approved-scope audit** for small processors that do not use integrated procurement/QMS software.
- Specification-management suites already connect formulations, allergens and labels. Generic label/spec management is therefore unattractive, but narrow revision-consistency checks may remain useful to spreadsheet-based small processors.
- Produce/farm software already covers cold-storage movements, harvest records, CSA/wholesale and field-to-shipment traceability.
