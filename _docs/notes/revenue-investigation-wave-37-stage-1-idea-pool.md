# Revenue Investigation — Wave 37 Stage 1 Idea Pool

## Platform thesis
monday.com Marketplace provides native subscriptions and billing. New Marketplace apps must use monday monetization; developers retain all revenue until an app reaches US$200,000 lifetime accumulated revenue, after which revenue share is 85% developer / 15% monday.com.

## Constraint
Generate 60 narrow monday.com apps. Avoid generic CRM, time tracking, forms, dashboards, project templates, broad reporting, AI assistants and generic automation builders. Prefer read-only preflights and lifecycle-risk utilities. Stop at Stage 4 planning.
