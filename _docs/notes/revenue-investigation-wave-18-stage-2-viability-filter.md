# Wave 18 Stage 2 — Viability Filter

## Evidence that changed scoring

- BC referral administration is a real burden, but the provincial Digital Referrals & Orders program and Ocean/Pathways ecosystem already target referral completeness, tracking and missing-information follow-up. Generic referral-management ideas are therefore crowded.
- MSP billing/rejection management is already served by dedicated BC billing platforms such as ClinicAid, so another full billing product is unattractive.
- BC MSP billing rules still create narrow QA opportunities around MVA identification, referral-dependent billing and evidence retention because practitioners remain accountable for correct claim coding and audit support.
- WorkSafeBC physiotherapy workflows require specific report forms and submission paths, while ICBC provider programs have their own operational requirements. Small allied-health clinics still manage claim numbers, report deadlines, extensions and billing handoffs across separate payer systems.
- Pharmacy prescription-transfer recordkeeping has explicit administrative record requirements, but pharmacy software is highly regulated and integrated, reducing appeal for a standalone tool.
